### Theme

The website will automatically pick a theme based on your OS/browser settings.
To test or develop a specific setting without changing your OS'/browser's
default:

1. Open DevTools
1. Click `Ctrl+Shift+P` to open the command palette
1. Choose the `Show Rendering` command
1. In the newly opened tab, pick the theme variant in
   `Emulate CSS media feature prefers-color-scheme` that should be rendered
